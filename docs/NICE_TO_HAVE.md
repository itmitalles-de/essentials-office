# Essentials+ Office nice-to-have ideas

These are ideas only. They are not approved scope, implementation stubs,
dependencies, or evidence that a module exists. None may displace the open
operational and recovery gates.

- Automate the existing Collabora WOPI create/open/edit/save/reload journey in
  a disposable environment.
- Add stable post-restore assertions for existing HR Lite and Intranet Lite
  objects when their supported APIs make the checks deterministic.
- Exercise an existing Nextcloud Mail account against the synthetic TLS
  IMAP/SMTP fixture without installing a mail platform.
- Add richer metric freshness and deliberately injected update-failure tests.
- Produce a privacy-reviewed external evidence archive with signatures and
  retention after an operator and retention policy are assigned.

HPB, mailcow installation, OIDC/SSO rollout, new HR/Intranet/Vaultwarden
features, migrations, Kubernetes, and additional modules are not backlog items
for this document.
